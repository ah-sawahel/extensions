# RTLy Pro: terms of sale and refunds

Effective date: 15 November 2026

These terms cover buying RTLy Pro, the optional paid upgrade of the RTLy browser extension. RTLy itself, including its reading fixes on every supported site and Markdown export, is free and doesn't need Pro.

**Who sells Pro.** Pro is sold through Polar (polar.sh), which acts as the merchant of record: Polar is the seller on your receipt, takes the payment, and handles sales tax, VAT and invoices. Polar's own checkout terms also apply to your purchase. RTLy is made by Sawa, who provides the license, the extension and support.

**What Pro gives you.** Unlimited Word exports, instead of 3 free Word exports a day, and any Pro features added later at no extra cost. Pro doesn't change what's free: reading fixes and Markdown export stay free.

**Plans and prices.** Prices are in US dollars and include taxes; Polar's checkout shows the final amount before you pay.
- **Yearly:** $12 a year (that's $1/month, billed $12 yearly). It renews automatically each year until you cancel.
- **Lifetime:** $19, paid once. "Lifetime" means for as long as RTLy is offered.

**Your license key.** After you pay, Polar emails you a license key. One key works on up to 3 browsers at a time. You can free a browser in RTLy (Settings → Pro → Deactivate this browser) or in your Polar account, and then use the key on another browser. The key is for you; please don't share or resell it.

**Renewal and cancelling (Yearly).** You can cancel at any time in your Polar account ("Manage subscription" in RTLy opens it). After you cancel, Pro keeps working until the end of the year you've paid for, and then RTLy returns to the free plan. You won't be charged again.

**Refunds: 14 days, no questions asked.** If Pro isn't right for you, email ah.sawahel+support@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase (or of a yearly renewal), with the email address you used at checkout, and we'll have Polar refund you in full. You don't have to give a reason. A refund ends the license: Pro stops on every browser the next time RTLy checks the key, usually within a day. Outside the 14 days, write to us anyway; we'll look at each case fairly. These terms don't limit any rights you have under the consumer law of your country.

**If RTLy stops being offered.** If we ever decide to stop offering RTLy, we'll announce it on the changelog page (https://ah-sawahel.github.io/extensions/rtly/changelog.html) at least 30 days before Pro stops working, and no further yearly renewals will be charged.

**How the license is checked.** RTLy checks your key with Polar at most about once a day, and only the key and the browser details listed in the privacy policy are sent: https://ah-sawahel.github.io/extensions/rtly/privacy.html. If RTLy can't reach Polar, Pro keeps working for 7 days, then pauses until the next successful check. Free features keep working throughout.

**Support.** ah.sawahel+support@gmail.com. We aim to answer within a few working days.

**Changes to these terms.** We may update these terms; the effective date above shows the latest version. Changes apply to purchases and renewals made after that date. A change never reduces what you've already paid for.
