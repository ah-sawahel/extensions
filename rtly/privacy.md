# RTLy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026
Applies to: the RTLy browser extension, version 1.0 and later.

**Summary.** RTLy doesn't collect, sell or share your chats or your browsing data. It works inside your browser. Its only network request is a license-key check for people who buy the optional Pro upgrade, made only after you enter a key, and that check sends only the license details described below.

**Page text.** RTLy runs only on the AI chat websites listed in its menu and on its support page. On those pages it reads the text of chat messages to tell whether each paragraph is written right-to-left (such as Arabic, Persian or Urdu) or left-to-right. It then sets each paragraph's direction and alignment. This happens inside your browser. The text isn't saved, logged or sent anywhere, and nobody, including us, can see it. RTLy doesn't read what you type in the message box; there, it only applies styling so that each line aligns with the language you type. RTLy can't run on any other website.

**Exports.** When you export a chat, RTLy reads the messages of that chat from the page and builds the Markdown or Word file inside your browser. The chat content passes only between RTLy's own parts, held in your browser's memory for at most a few minutes, and the file is saved where your browser saves downloads. Nothing is uploaded. The files carry no RTLy branding or tracking marks.

**What RTLy stores.** On your device, in the browser's extension storage:
- your settings: whether RTLy is on, the on/off switch for each supported site, your font choice and interface language, and whether you've closed the welcome card;
- a count of today's Word exports, with one-way codes for the chats you exported today (no chat text, titles or page addresses), reset every day;
- for Pro users, the license key, its plan and status, the activation ID Polar gave this browser, and when it was last checked.

This data isn't synced to other devices and is deleted when you remove RTLy. RTLy never stores chat text, chat titles, page addresses or browsing history.

**The current tab's address.** When you click the RTLy button, the browser lets RTLy see the address of the tab you're on, for that click only. RTLy uses it to show whether it works on that site, and to export the chat on it if you ask. If RTLy doesn't work on that site, it uses the address to fill in the "Request this site" email with the site's name. The address isn't stored or sent.

**Network requests.** RTLy has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking. It doesn't load code or settings from the internet: everything it uses is inside the extension. The only network request it ever makes is the Pro license check described below, and only after you enter a license key.

**Pro license.** RTLy's core features are free. If you buy Pro and enter your license key in RTLy, it contacts our payment and licensing provider, Polar (polar.sh), to activate the key on this browser and to check it again from time to time. Each request sends only:
- the license key;
- our Polar account identifier;
- when activating, a short label for this browser made of its name and operating system (for example "Chrome · macOS");
- when checking or deactivating, the activation ID Polar gave this browser.

Nothing else is sent: no chat text, page addresses or browsing data. RTLy checks the key at most about once a day, when your browser starts or when you start a Word export, and whenever you press "Check now". Like any website, Polar also sees your IP address and the standard information your browser sends with any web request. Where your browser asks for permission before an add-on sends authentication information and technical data (the key, and your browser's and operating system's names), RTLy asks at the moment you activate the key.

Removing RTLy deletes the key from your browser but doesn't free that browser's activation. To move Pro to another browser, deactivate it first in RTLy (Settings → Pro), or manage your activations in your Polar account.

**Buying Pro.** "Buy" opens Polar's checkout as a normal web page in a new tab; RTLy sends nothing to it. Polar sells Pro as the merchant of record: it processes your payment details, email address and billing country under its own privacy policy (https://polar.sh/legal/privacy-policy). We receive your email address and order details from Polar so we can provide your license, handle refunds and answer support requests. We never see your full card details. Terms of sale and refunds: https://ah-sawahel.github.io/extensions/rtly/terms.html

**Emails you choose to send.** "Report a broken site" and "Request this site" open your own email app with a message addressed to us. The message is filled in with only:
- the site's host name (for example chatgpt.com);
- RTLy's version;
- your browser and operating system.

It never includes chat text, chat titles or full page addresses. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to fix problems. We don't share it with anyone, apart from the email service that hosts our mailbox. We delete support emails 12 months after the last reply, and sooner if you ask.

**Traces on the page.** While RTLy is on, it adds direction and marker attributes to the message elements of the supported websites, and removes them when you switch it off. A website could notice these attributes and infer that RTLy is installed. RTLy doesn't send the website anything.

**Third parties.** RTLy's only third party is Polar, and only for Pro purchases and license checks as described above. We don't sell or share data with anyone else. The add-on store you installed RTLy from may process data about your installation under its own privacy policy. We only see the totals that the store gives developers, such as the number of installs.

**Children.** RTLy isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

**Your choices.** You can switch RTLy off for one site or everywhere, or remove it at any time; removing it deletes everything it stored on your device. If you never enter a license key, RTLy makes no network requests at all. For Pro, we hold your email address and order details (received from Polar) and any emails you've sent us. To access, correct or delete them, write to ah.sawahel+support@gmail.com. Polar keeps its own records of your purchase as the seller; its privacy policy explains how to reach it.

**Changes.** If a future version changes how RTLy handles data, we'll update this policy and its effective date before that version is released, and say so in the release notes.
