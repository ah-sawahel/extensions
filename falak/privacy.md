# Falak Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 October 2026
**Last updated:** 20 October 2026

Falak ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Sawa. Package: com.sawa.falak. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: Falak has no accounts and no server of ours. Your location, sensor readings and camera images are used on your device only. The Google services listed below (ads and purchases) have their own data practices, which we describe as accurately as we can.

## 1. What we do not do

- We do not run a backend and do not receive or store your data on servers we operate.
- There are no accounts, sign-in, or profiles.
- We do not send your location, your saved place, your sensor readings or your camera images to us or to anyone.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Permissions the app uses

- **Approximate location:** only when you tap the GPS button. Foreground only, never in the background. If you refuse, pick a city by name.
- **Camera:** only when you open the camera view (Pro). Never in the background. If you refuse, the rest of the app works as usual.
- **Notifications (Android 13 and later):** only if you turn on the Hilal Night reminder or sky alerts. The app also uses the "run at startup" and "keep awake" permissions so scheduled reminders survive a restart, and vibration for the on-target signal in the crescent hunt.
- **Internet and network state:** used by the ads and purchase components. The sky, Moon, Hijri date, Hilal and event features work offline.
- **Advertising ID and Android ad services:** the ads component may read your Android advertising ID and use Android's privacy-preserving ad services, subject to your consent and your Android settings.
- **Google Play Billing:** used to buy and restore Pro.

## 4. Third parties

These services run inside the app and receive data directly from your device. We do not control them. Their policies apply to what they collect.

- **Google AdMob (ads) and Google's User Messaging Platform (consent).** Shows ads in the free version, never on the sky view or the Hilal screens. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and similar regions), the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, and uses personalized ads only if you agree. Without that consent, ads may be limited or non-personalized where the law allows. Pro removes ads.
  - How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  - Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure
  - Manage ad personalization: https://myadcenter.google.com
- **Google Play Billing (Pro purchase).** The purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive only the purchase result (whether Pro is unlocked) on your device; we do not see your payment details. https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

## 5. Diagnostics

The current version of Falak contains no crash-reporting or analytics component and sends no diagnostics. Settings shows a "Share diagnostics" switch that is off by default; it has no effect in this version. If a future version adds diagnostics, it will stay off until you allow it, it will be used only to fix problems and improve the app (never for advertising), and we will update this policy before that version is released.

## 6. Children

Falak is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under the higher age that applies in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. Because we hold no accounts and run no server, we have nothing to delete on our side. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help; ad and purchase data held by Google is managed through Google.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Because we do not hold your personal data, most choices are on your device: revoke permissions, clear app data, uninstall, reset your advertising ID, change ad consent. If you are in the EEA, the UK or a similar region you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection and portability, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. For data held by Google, use Google's tools (https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy). For anything we hold (such as support emails), contact us.

**Legal basis (EEA and UK):** consent for personalized ads; performance of a contract for processing your purchase (by Google Play); legitimate interest for replying to your messages.

## 8. Security and transfers

Data stored by the app stays in its private storage on your device. Google's components communicate over encrypted connections. Google may process data in countries other than yours, under its own policies and transfer safeguards.

## 9. Changes

We will update this page when the app's practices change and change the "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
