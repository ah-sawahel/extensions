# Falak: Stargaze & Hilal

Offline-first, Arabic-first stargazing app for Android. Point your phone at the sky to find stars, planets and constellations, follow the Moon and the Hijri date, and use Hilal Night each month to find the new crescent after sunset, with two published visibility criteria side by side. Predictions are scientific estimates; official month dates come from your country's authority.
