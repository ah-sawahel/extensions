# Falak support

Questions, a bug, or a wrong date or time? Email **ah.sawahel+support@gmail.com**. Tell us your phone model, your city and what you saw.

## Common questions

**Restore Pro on a new phone.** Pro is a one-time purchase. Sign in to the same Google Play or Apple account, then tap Restore purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen.

**Why does my Hijri date differ from my country's?** Falak shows a calculated date. You can move it by a day with Adjust Hijri date. Official month dates come from your country's authority.

**Does Hilal Night tell me when the month starts?** No. It shows scientific predictions from two published visibility criteria, not a ruling.

**Does Falak need internet?** The sky, Moon, Hijri date, Hilal and event features work offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

**Do I have to share my location?** No. Pick a city by name instead. If you use GPS, your location stays on your device.

**Remove ads.** Falak Pro removes all ads. Ad consent choices are under Privacy options in Settings, where shown.

**Reminders do not arrive.** Check that notifications are allowed for Falak in your phone's settings, and that the reminder is on in Settings.
