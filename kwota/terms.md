# Kwota Pro: terms of sale and refunds

Effective date: 16 November 2026

**Who sells what.** Kwota is a browser extension published by Sawa. Its core features are free. Kwota Pro is an optional upgrade sold through Polar (polar.sh), which acts as the **merchant of record**: Polar takes the payment, handles sales tax and VAT, and issues your receipt or invoice. Sawa provides the license and support.

**Plans and prices.**
- **Pro Yearly:** $12 per year, shown as "$1/month, billed $12 yearly". It is charged once a year and renews automatically each year until you cancel. There is no monthly charge.
- **Pro Lifetime:** $19, paid once. "Lifetime" means for as long as Kwota is offered.

Prices are in US dollars and **include taxes**; the checkout shows the final amount before you pay.

**What you get.** A license key, sent by Polar by email, that unlocks Pro in Kwota: bulk export to Markdown, JSON and Word files, archive to file, and search tools (filters and bulk actions on search results), plus every future Pro feature for as long as your license is active. One key works on **up to 3 browsers** at a time. You can free a browser with "Deactivate this browser" in Kwota's settings, or in your Polar account.

**Cancelling.** You can cancel a yearly plan at any time in your Polar account (the link is in Kwota's settings and in Polar's emails). Pro keeps working until the end of the year you paid for, then stops renewing.

**Refunds.** If Pro isn't right for you, write to us within **14 days** of a purchase or a yearly renewal and you'll get a full refund, no questions asked. Outside the 14 days, write to us anyway; we'll look at each case fairly. A refund ends the license: Pro switches off at Kwota's next license check (usually within a day). This doesn't affect any rights you have under the consumer law of your country.

**License checks.** Kwota checks the key with Polar at most about once a day. If the check can't reach Polar, Pro keeps working for 7 days, then pauses until a check succeeds. What the check sends is described in the privacy policy.

**Free features stay free.** The usage meter, alerts, reset reminders, history, search, clean-up with Delete and single-chat Markdown/JSON export never need Pro, and a refund, cancellation or paused license never affects them.

**Deleting chats.** Clean up and Archive to file delete chats from your claude.ai account only after you confirm the exact number. claude.ai can't restore deleted chats, and neither can Kwota. Archive to file saves the chats to a file before deleting them: keep that file, because it is then your only copy. Kwota isn't responsible for chats deleted after you confirmed the deletion, or for files you delete or lose.

**The service Kwota works with.** Kwota works with claude.ai and depends on how claude.ai works. If claude.ai changes, a feature may stop working until Kwota is updated; we aim to fix such problems quickly. Kwota is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

**If Kwota is discontinued.** We'll give at least 30 days' notice in the changelog before Pro stops working, and refund yearly plans renewed within the last 14 days.

**Changes to these terms.** We may update these terms; the new version applies to purchases and renewals after its effective date, and we'll note changes in the changelog.
