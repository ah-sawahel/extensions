# Kwota Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026
Applies to: the Kwota browser extension, version 1.0 and later.

**Summary.** Kwota doesn't collect, sell or share your chats or browsing data. It works inside your browser, only on claude.ai, as you. Apart from claude.ai itself, its only network connection is a license check for people who buy the optional Pro upgrade, and that check sends only the license details described below. Nothing is sent to us.

**What Kwota reads on claude.ai, and why.** Kwota runs only on claude.ai. While you have claude.ai open, Kwota asks claude.ai, from that page and as the signed-in user, for:
- your usage numbers (the percentages and reset times claude.ai shows under Settings › Usage) and the identifier of the active organisation, to show the meter, alerts, forecast and history;
- your chat list (titles, projects, dates and whether a chat is starred or archived), only when you open Clean up or Export, to show and filter it;
- search results for the words you type, only when you press Search (or ask for more results). Kwota sends your query to claude.ai's own search, the same search claude.ai offers on its site, and shows the matching chats with a short snippet;
- the messages of the chats you export or archive, only when you start that export, to build the files.

This happens inside your browser. Chat lists, search queries, search results and chat messages are held in memory only while you use them; Kwota never saves them or sends them anywhere else. Kwota doesn't read your claude.ai login cookie or any other cookie, doesn't read what you type in the message box, and never sends messages or prompts for you. To refresh the meter after a reply, Kwota notices when claude.ai finishes a reply from the timing of the page's own requests, not from their content.

**Requests to claude.ai.** These are ordinary requests from your signed-in session, made at a limited rate (at most about one per second for lists, searches and exports) and only while claude.ai is open in your browser. With no claude.ai tab open, Kwota makes no requests to claude.ai. Anthropic receives these requests as it receives your own use of claude.ai, under its own privacy policy.

**Changes you ask for.** When you delete chats with Clean up, Kwota asks claude.ai to delete exactly the chats you confirmed, after showing you their number. With "Archive to file" (Pro), Kwota first saves the chats to a file, and deletes them only after the file is saved and you confirm the deletion. claude.ai can't restore deleted chats, so neither can we; keep the file. Kwota changes nothing on claude.ai without your confirmation.

**Files you export.** Exports (Markdown, JSON and, with Pro, Word files, one file or a ZIP of several) are made inside your browser and saved through your browser's own download or save dialog, to your device. Nothing is uploaded, and we never receive them. Files carry no Kwota branding.

**What Kwota stores.** On your device, in the browser's extension storage:
- your settings (meter and badge switches, alert thresholds, reset reminders);
- your latest usage readings and those in the current usage windows (percentages, reset times and when each was read), used for the forecast and to send each alert only once;
- daily usage peaks for the last 90 days, deleted automatically after that;
- the identifier of your active Claude organisation (a code, not its name);
- for Pro users only: the license key, its plan and status, and when it was last checked.

Kwota also keeps short-lived working data in memory, such as which claude.ai tabs are open, when it last refreshed and whether an export is running; it's cleared when the browser closes. Kwota never stores chat content, chat titles, search queries, names, email addresses or browsing history, keeps no count of your searches, and syncs nothing to other devices. "Clear history" in Kwota's settings deletes the history at once; removing Kwota deletes everything it stored.

**Notifications.** Usage alerts and reset reminders are created on your device from the stored readings. You can switch each one off.

**Pro license.** Kwota's core features are free. If you buy Pro and enter your license key in Kwota, it sends these details to our payment and licensing provider, Polar (polar.sh), to activate the key, to re-check it at most about once a day (when your browser starts or before a Pro action, and when you press "Check now"), and to deactivate it when you ask:
- the key;
- our account identifier at Polar;
- when activating, a short label made of your browser's name and operating system (for example "Chrome · macOS"), so you can recognise the browser in your Polar account;
- after activation, the activation identifier Polar gave this browser.

Nothing else is sent: no chat content, chat titles, search queries, usage numbers or browsing data. Like any website, Polar also sees your IP address when Kwota contacts it. Where your browser asks for permission before an add-on sends this kind of data (authentication information and technical data), Kwota asks at the moment you activate the key. Removing Kwota deletes the key from your browser but doesn't free its activation: deactivate it in Kwota's settings first, or manage activations in your Polar account.

**Buying Pro.** "Buy Pro" opens Polar's checkout as a normal web page in a new tab; Kwota sends nothing to it. You pay on Polar's page. Polar processes your payment details and email address as the merchant of record, under its own privacy policy (https://polar.sh/legal/privacy-policy). We receive your email address and order details from Polar so we can provide your license and support. The terms of sale are at https://ah-sawahel.github.io/extensions/kwota/terms.html.

**No other connections.** Kwota has no servers, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't load code or settings from the internet: everything it uses is inside the extension. Apart from claude.ai, the only request it ever makes is the Pro license check described above, and only after you enter a license key.

**Emails you choose to send.** "Report a problem" opens our support page or a message in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to fix problems, and share it with no one apart from the email service that hosts our mailbox. We delete support emails 12 months after the last reply, and sooner if you ask.

**Traces on the page.** Kwota adds its own small elements to claude.ai pages: the meter and, while it's open, the Chats panel. claude.ai could notice them and infer that Kwota is installed. Kwota doesn't change claude.ai's own content.

**Third parties.** Kwota's only third party is Polar, and only for Pro license checks and purchases as described above. The add-on store you installed Kwota from may process data about your installation under its own privacy policy; we only see the totals stores give developers, such as the number of installs.

**Children.** Kwota isn't directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone through the extension.

**Your choices.** You can switch off the in-page meter, the toolbar number and each alert, clear the usage history, deactivate or remove a license key, or remove Kwota at any time. Apart from emails you've sent us and the order details Polar shares with us when you buy Pro, we hold no personal data about you. To ask about those, write to ah.sawahel+support@gmail.com.

**Changes.** If a future version changes how Kwota handles data, we'll update this policy and its effective date before that version is released, and say so in the release notes.

**Independence.** Kwota is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. The name Claude is used only to say which service Kwota works with.
